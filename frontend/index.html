📜 RULES

RULE 1 — FOOD AT HOME FIRST. Before buying restaurant food: check this app.
RULE 2 — PICK FROM THE LIST. Don’t sit around trying to invent what to eat. Open WHAT CAN I MAKE? Pick something.
RULE 3 — PREPPED FOOD WINS. If a meal is already prepared, eat that before creating more work.
RULE 4 — RESTAURANT FOOD IS NOT THE DEFAULT. Home food = normal. Eating out = optional.
RULE 5 — EASY BEATS PERFECT. Chicken tenders + fries at home is better than spending $25 because cooking something complicated felt like too much.
RULE 6 — KEEP FOOD I ACTUALLY LIKE. Don’t build a food list around fantasy meals I won’t eat.
RULE 7 — RUNNING LOW = ADD IT. If something is almost gone, mark OUT or ADD TO SHOPPING. Don’t wait until there is nothing to eat.
